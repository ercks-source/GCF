Historic Renovation

This sensitive renovation project required us to blend new construction with the existing historic structure seamlessly. We carefully assessed the original timber framing, reinforcing where necessary while preserving the building's architectural character. The addition incorporated modern building techniques while using materials and methods that complemented the original construction. Special attention was paid to matching timber dimensions, replicating traditional joinery details, and ensuring the new framing integrated invisibly with the historic elements. This project demonstrated our ability to respect architectural heritage while meeting current building codes and structural requirements.

Multi-Family Development

This 12-unit townhome complex showcased our ability to execute repetitive framing with precision and efficiency. Each unit featured identical floor plans, allowing us to develop a systematic approach that maximized quality while minimizing construction time. We implemented advanced framing techniques including optimized stud spacing and engineered lumber where appropriate to reduce material waste and improve energy efficiency. The project required careful coordination of utilities and fire-rated assemblies between units. Our experienced crew completed the framing phase ahead of schedule, demonstrating the value of systematic planning and skilled execution in multi-family construction.

Industrial Warehouse

This 25,000 square foot warehouse facility required heavy-duty structural steel framing designed to support significant roof loads and potential future equipment installations. We installed wide-flange steel beams spanning up to 60 feet, creating a column-free interior space that maximizes usable floor area. The project included specialized framing for overhead crane support, reinforced loading dock areas, and provisions for future mezzanine expansion. All work was performed to exacting tolerances, ensuring proper alignment for the eventual installation of roof panels and exterior cladding. This project highlighted our capabilities in large-scale commercial and industrial construction.

Mountain Retreat

This custom mountain vacation home featured exposed timber framing as a key architectural element. We hand-selected premium Douglas fir timbers and employed traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery techniques to create dramatic exposed trusses and posts throughout the great room. The project combined heavy timber construction with conventional framing in a way that highlighted the natural beauty of the wood while meeting all structural requirements. Each timber was carefully milled, fitted, and finished to showcase the natural grain and character of the wood. The result is a warm, inviting space that celebrates traditional craftsmanship while incorporating modern building science for energy efficiency and durability.
